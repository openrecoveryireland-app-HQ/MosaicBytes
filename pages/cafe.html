Mosaic Recovery-Friendly Cafés

A place to go. A place to connect.

Mosaic Recovery-Friendly Cafés is a growing nationwide network of cafés offering a welcoming, alcohol-free place where people in recovery can have a coffee, meet someone, or simply spend time away from alcohol-centred environments.

A Simple Welcome

Participating cafés are not being asked to organise meetings, provide supervision, reserve private space, or offer discounts.

They are simply choosing one or more regular times during the week when people in recovery will be especially welcome.
